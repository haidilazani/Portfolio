Drawing an interactive ASCII terrain in vanilla JS

The background of this site is not an image and not a library. It's two value-noise fields cross-faded to make terrain breathe, drawn as a grid of ASCII characters picked by elevation, with every move and click added as a ripple so the characters swell around your pointer. Here's the whole algorithm, case by case, and why touch devices and reduced-motion users get one static render instead.
